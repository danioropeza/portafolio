description: 'Comments are not “pure good”. Indeed, comments are, at best, a necessary evil. The proper use of comments is to compensate for our failure to express ourself in code. Comments are always failures. So when you find yourself in a position where you need to write a comment, think it through and see whether there isn’t some way to turn the tables and express yourself in code. Comments lie. Not always, and not intentionally, but too often. The older a comment is, and the farther away it is from the code it describes, the more likely it is to be just plain wrong. The reason is simple. Programmers can’t realistically maintain them.'
